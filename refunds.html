Refund Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Fourteen days, no questions asked. And if you bought early access for a feature that never ships, you get your money back whenever you ask.

14-day refund

You can have a full refund within 14 days of purchase, on request, no questions asked and no reason needed. Email kodeksidev@gmail.com with your order number.

Early access guarantee

Early access is sold on features that are still being built, so it comes with a longer guarantee. If a Pro feature that was promised at the time of your purchase has not shipped within six months of that purchase, you can have a full refund on request, at any time. The 14-day limit does not apply to this.

How refunds are processed

Refunds are handled by Paddle, the merchant of record, and are returned to the original payment method. Your confirmation comes from Paddle. The money usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

After a refund

The Pro licence key stops working, and the app returns to the free version, which stays free and fully usable.

Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to any refund rights you have under consumer law where you live. It does not replace them.

Contact

To request a refund, email kodeksidev@gmail.com and include the order number from your Paddle receipt. See also the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
